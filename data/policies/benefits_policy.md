# Benefits Policy

Health insurance coverage for employees starts on the first day of the month after the start date. The company pays 90 percent of the premium for the employee and 70 percent for dependents.

The company offers a 401(k) retirement plan with a 4 percent employer match. Employees become fully vested in the match after 3 years of service.

Each employee receives an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not roll over.

The wellness program reimburses up to 50 USD per month for gym memberships or fitness classes.

Employees are eligible for the employee stock purchase plan after 6 months of employment, with a 10 percent discount on the share price.
