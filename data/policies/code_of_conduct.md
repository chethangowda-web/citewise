# Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying of any kind are strictly prohibited and may result in termination.

Employees must disclose any conflict of interest to their manager and HR in writing. Accepting gifts worth more than 75 USD from vendors or customers is not allowed.

Concerns can be reported to HR or anonymously through the ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0100. The company prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

Employees may not engage in outside work that competes with the company or uses company time or resources. Side projects must be approved by a manager.

Insider trading is prohibited. Employees must not trade company or partner stock while in possession of material non-public information.
