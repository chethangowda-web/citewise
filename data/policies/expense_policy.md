# Expense Policy

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business expenses incurred on behalf of the company. All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date through the finance portal, with original receipts attached.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance in economy class. Flights longer than 8 hours may be booked in premium economy with manager approval.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 180 USD per night, or 250 USD per night in New York, San Francisco and London.

Any single expense above 500 USD requires written pre-approval from the department head. Expenses submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.

Approved reimbursements are paid within 10 business days directly to the employee's bank account.
