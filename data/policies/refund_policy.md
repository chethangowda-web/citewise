# Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the purchase date. Refund requests must include the original order number.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the request is approved.

Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable once the license key or card code has been delivered. Exceptions apply where required by law.

Items returned after 30 days may be eligible for store credit at the discretion of the support manager, up to 12 months after purchase.

Shipping costs are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was shipped.
