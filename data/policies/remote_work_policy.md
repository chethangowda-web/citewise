# Remote Work Policy

Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams agree on shared in-office days, typically Tuesday and Thursday.

Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a department head and are reviewed every 6 months.

The company provides a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD for fully remote employees and 200 USD for hybrid employees.

Remote employees must be available during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone, and must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems.

Working from another country for more than 14 days in a calendar year requires prior approval from HR because of tax and legal implications.
