# Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next calendar year.

Vacation requests of more than 5 consecutive days must be submitted at least 14 days in advance and approved by the employee's manager.

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave: primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave, and secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave may begin up to 2 weeks before the expected birth or adoption date.

Bereavement leave: up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member and 2 paid days for extended family.
