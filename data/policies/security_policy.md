# Information Security Policy

All company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatically lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Employees must never share their passwords with anyone, including IT staff.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and all cloud systems.

Suspected phishing emails must be reported to security@company.example within 1 hour using the Report Phishing button. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious messages.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 2 hours of discovery so the device can be remotely wiped.

Confidential data may only be stored on company-approved systems. Using personal cloud storage or personal email for company data is prohibited.

Security incidents are classified as low, medium or critical. Critical incidents are escalated to the CISO immediately and require a written report within 48 hours.
